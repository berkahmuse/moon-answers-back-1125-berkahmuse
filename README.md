Entry for [The Moon Finally Answers Back](https://make.fun/bounty/u-mang-the-moon-finally-answers-back-781a7ec1) on make.fun.

`main.ts` writes one original single-line comeback, in the Moon's own voice, to `/output/entry.txt`.

> Night shift, four billion years, no breaks, no pay — and you still howl your wishes at me like I'm the manager.

111 characters, one line. The Moon here is not a romantic muse or a neighbour — it is a night-shift worker four billion years into a shift with no breaks and no pay, answering the howling and the wishing with deadpan exhaustion. The turn lands at the end: the people staring up are not devotees, they are customers treating the Moon like the manager on duty. Wry, old, and tired of being wished upon — no explanation, the complaint is the joke.

## Run

```sh
node main.ts   # writes /output/entry.txt
```
